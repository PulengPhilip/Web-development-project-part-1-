# Bright Spark Electrical Services – Part 1 Research Notes

Bright Spark Electrical Services is a fictional small electrical business created for an academic website project.

## Business problem
The scenario states that the business relies on Facebook and word-of-mouth. Its Facebook information is outdated and clients have no enquiry option outside business hours. The website addresses this through updated service information, a quote form, visible contact details and a professional company profile.

## Target audience
- Residential property owners
- Small business owners
- Property managers and landlords
- Customers requiring routine electrical work
- Customers requiring emergency call-outs

## Content to source before production
- Verified owner/team information
- Qualifications and registrations
- Service area and business address
- Real phone/email/WhatsApp details
- Completed project photographs
- Customer reviews
- Emergency operating hours
- Google Maps location

## Image sourcing
Use original or properly licensed images and record creator, source and licence information in the reference list.

**Academic note:** Bright Spark Electrical Services is fictional. All contact details, location data and credentials shown in the site are placeholders.
